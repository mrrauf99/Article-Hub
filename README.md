## What you can do

### Read and discover
- Browse published articles by topic.
- Search for stories by title or keyword.
- Read articles in a clear, easy-to-follow format.
- Save articles to a personal reading list and remove them whenever you like.

### Write and publish
- Create articles with headings and formatted text.
- Add a cover image.
- Save work in progress and make changes later.
- Send finished articles to the editorial team for review.
- See the status of your articles and manage your drafts from your writer area.

### Review and manage articles
- Review articles submitted by writers.
- Approve articles for everyone to read or reject them.
- Manage articles across the platform.
- Browse member profiles and manage account roles.

### Manage your account
- Create an account and confirm your email address.
- Sign in with your email or Google account.
- Reset a forgotten password.
- Add a short biography, social links, and a profile picture.
- Turn on an extra sign-in check for added account security.

## How publishing works

1. A writer creates an article and saves it while working.
2. The writer submits it for review.
3. An administrator reviews the article and approves or rejects it.
4. Approved articles appear for readers to discover and enjoy.
